What this is

A tiny web app that turns any picture into a 1-bit black-and-white image, the way the first Macintosh drew pictures in 1984.

How it works

Each pixel becomes black or white. The difference between its real shade and the chosen one is the "error". Atkinson dithering passes 1/8 of that error to six nearby pixels and simply drops the rest. Dropping part of the error is what gives the look its crisp highlights and deep shadows.

Everything runs in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

Why

Made to honour the late Bill Atkinson, who wrote QuickDraw, MacPaint and HyperCard, and came up with this dithering method for the original Mac.
